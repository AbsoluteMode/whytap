# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work,
and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow the Covenant, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious
violations.

Report unacceptable behaviour to security@whytap.ai. Reports are handled
confidentially.
